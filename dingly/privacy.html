Dingly Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 October 2026

Dingly ("the app") is a multi-timer app published by BDA Apps Studio ("we"). This policy explains what data the app handles.

What we collect

We do not collect, store or receive any personal data. The app has no accounts, no sign-in and no servers of our own. Your timers, presets and settings are stored only on your device and are removed when you uninstall the app.

Advertising

The app shows ads provided by Google AdMob. We request non-personalized ads only. To serve and measure ads, Google may process data such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and ad interaction data, in line with Google's policies and Privacy Policy.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent through a Google-certified consent form before ads are requested. You can review or change your choice at any time in Settings → Ad privacy settings (shown when applicable). You can also reset your advertising ID in your Android settings.

Permissions

  • Notifications, foreground service and full-screen notification: to keep timers running in the background, show their remaining time with controls, and show the alarm screen when a timer ends.
  • Exact alarms: to ring at the exact moment a timer ends.
  • Vibration and audio: to alert you when a timer ends.
  • Internet access: used only to load ads.

None of these permissions are used to collect personal data.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

Data deletion

Since we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to delete. Uninstalling the app removes all locally stored data.

Changes

We may update this policy; the date at the top shows the latest version.
